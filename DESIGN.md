## Brand & Style

The design system is engineered to convey structural precision, absolute transparency, and Mediterranean architectural craftsmanship. It bridges high-end interior architecture with honest, dependable execution for homeowners and commercial property investors seeking renovation and refurbishment.

The design philosophy combines **Corporate Modern** rigor with **Architectural Minimalism**:
- **Trust & Clarity**: Every interaction emphasizes reliability, upfront pricing expectations, structural guarantees, and regulatory compliance.
- **Craftsmanship & Materiality**: Clean planes, crisp edges, subtle structural borders, and generous breathing room mirror architectural blueprints and modern finished interiors.
- **Conversion Discipline**: Primary energetic accents are strictly reserved for decisive high-converting actions (e.g., requesting quotes or initiating project assessments), preventing visual fatigue and maintaining authoritative composure.

## Colors

The palette establishes an architectural hierarchy using stark deep slates anchored by clinical off-white surfaces, complemented by an intentional dual-accent system.

- **Primary Anchor (`#0F172A`)**: Deep Slate Charcoal. Utilized for dominant headlines, primary structural framing, footers, high-contrast badges, and grounding elements.
- **Secondary Accent (`#EA580C`)**: Warm Terracotta Orange. Held exclusively for high-intent conversion actions ("Pedir presupuesto gratis", primary CTAs, urgent notification pips). It should never occupy more than 5% of viewport visual mass.
- **Tertiary Accent (`#0284C7`)**: Structural Cobalt. Dedicated to technical assurance: engineering guarantees, licensing badges, step progress indicators, interactive blueprint pins, and informational icons.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FFFFFF`, `#F8FAFC`, `#F1F5F9`)**: Layered architectural whites. Backdrops transition seamlessly from pure white `#FFFFFF` into cool tint `#F8FAFC` for content sections, with `#F1F5F9` serving as divider lines, neutral card fills, and skeleton states.
- **Body Neutral (`#334155`)**: Slate 700, balancing legibility and softness against clinical white backdrops.

## Typography

Typography relies on **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all levels. It delivers crisp, geometric structure while maintaining approachable humanities through subtle open counters and balanced curves.

- **Headings**: Set tight negative letter tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.03em`) and heavy weights (`700` to `800`) to project structural confidence and permanence.
- **Body & Paragraphs**: Provide spacious line heights (`1.5` to `1.6`) and neutral tracking for legal, architectural, and financial estimates.
- **Labels & Microcopy**: Use `label-caps` in uppercase with wide letter spacing (`+0.08em`) for phase counters, category kickers, square-meter specifications, and accreditation metadata.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a structured 12-column responsive fluid grid with strict maximum content boundary limits to preserve an editorial and architectural composition.

- **Grid Architecture**:
  - **Desktop (≥ 1024px)**: 12-column grid, max-width `1280px`, `gutter: 1.5rem`, `margin: 3rem`.
  - **Tablet (768px – 1023px)**: 8-column grid, fluid width, `gutter: 1.25rem`, `margin: 2rem`.
  - **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column grid, fluid width, `gutter: 1rem`, `margin: 1.25rem`.
- **Rhythm & Cadence**: Layout follows an 8px baseline rhythm. Major section transitions use intentional vertical whitespace (`4rem` to `6rem`) framed by subtle hairlines (`1px solid #E2E8F0`) to evoke architectural blueprint guidelines.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses diffuse atmospheric ambient shadows paired with low-contrast structural outlines rather than heavy skeuomorphic drop shadows.

- **Ground Level (Flat Surfaces)**: Background canvas uses `#FFFFFF` or `#F8FAFC`. Contained units carry an explicit 1px outline of `#E2E8F0` or `#F1F5F9`.
- **Level 1 (Cards, Trust Items, Form Pods)**: Resting state combines a 1px border (`#E2E8F0`) with a diffuse ambient shadow: `0px 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0px 8px 24px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Hover, Comparison Modules)**: Raised state transitions smoothly via a subtle upward translation (`translateY(-2px)`) and deepened ambient spread: `0px 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0px 20px 32px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Overlays, Sticky Booking Panels)**: High floating components feature a 1px border tinted in `#CBD5E1` combined with deep elevation: `0px 25px 50px -12px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.18)`. Glassmorphic sticky headers use backdrop-filter blur (`12px`) overlaid atop `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)`.

## Shapes

The geometric framework balances architectural discipline with approachable modern craft. 

- **Containers & Cards**: Styled at `rounded-lg` (16px / `1rem`), creating comfortable framing for high-resolution project photography and financial calculators.
- **Interactive Controls (Inputs, Dropdowns)**: Defined at standard `rounded` (8px / `0.5rem`) for precise tactile familiarity.
- **Buttons & Micro-Badges**: Formed as soft squircles (`0.5rem` to `0.75rem`) or full rounded pills (`9999px`) for conversion-oriented micro-actions, guaranteeing maximum touch friendliness across mobile interfaces.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Conversion CTA**: Pill or squircle (`rounded-lg`), bold (`fontWeight: 700`), filled with Secondary Orange (`#EA580C`), crisp white text, accompanied by an intentional hover lift and brightness shift (`#C2410C`). Applied strictly for high-conversion actions: *"Pedir presupuesto gratis"*, *"Calcular mi reforma"*.
- **Secondary Corporate Action**: Filled with Primary Charcoal (`#0F172A`), white text, hover state at `#1E293B`. Used for account access, catalog downloads, or secondary workflows.
- **Architectural Outline**: Transparent background, 1.5px border in `#CBD5E1`, text in `#0F172A`, turning to `#F1F5F9` background fill on hover. Used for portfolio filters and project spec browsing.

### Comparison Sliders (Before & After)
- Encapsulated within a `16px` border-radius overflow-hidden frame with a 1px perimeter line (`#E2E8F0`).
- Vertical divider line: 2px solid `#FFFFFF` with an integrated central pill handle (`40px` circular hub, `#0F172A` background, pure white dual bidirectional arrow icons, drop shadow `0 4px 12px rgba(0,0,0,0.25)`).
- Floating subtle badges in top corners: "ANTES" and "DESPUÉS" styled in `label-caps` using semi-translucent backdrop slate (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.6)`) with backdrop blur.

### Trust Bar & Guarantees
- Modular horizontal strip with `#F8FAFC` background and a 1px border.
- Features technical icons (safety shield, 10-year warranty, certified architects, fixed-date penalty clauses) finished in Tertiary Cobalt (`#0284C7`) inside circular `#E0F2FE` soft frames.
- Typography pairs `label-lg` with `body-sm` in slate gray for quick factual scanning.

### Process & Step-by-Step Progress Lines
- Horizontal rail on desktop, vertical timeline on mobile.
- Active path highlighted with 2px solid `#0284C7`; future stages in `#E2E8F0`.
- Numbered circular nodes (`32px x 32px`): Completed steps feature `#0284C7` background with a white checkmark; active step is outlined with double-ring focal accent; pending steps utilize `#F1F5F9` with `#64748B` numeral text.

### Cards (Project Showcase & Pricing Pods)
- **Portfolio Showcase**: Image aspect ratio 16:10, top corners rounded to `16px`, subtle bottom metadata container housing project scope, duration, and municipal location.
- **Budget Breakdown Pod**: Pure white background, 1px border (`#E2E8F0`), padded with `space-lg`. Line items alternate with transparent dividers and clear architectural icon stamps.

### Form Inputs & Fields
- Height `48px` to ensure touch ergonomics.
- Crisp border in `#CBD5E1`, neutral white canvas, inset text in `#0F172A`.
- Active focus state features an outline ring in `#0284C7` (2px thickness, `ring-offset-2`) without jarring chromatic shifts.
- Error validation uses clear architectural cinnabar red (`#DC2626`) with explicit inline descriptive tags.
